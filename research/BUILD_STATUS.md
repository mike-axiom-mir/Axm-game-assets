## Current interpretation

Game Asset Forge has moved from **architecture-only** to a real native manufacturing substrate.

It has **not** crossed the final quality boundary. The remaining problem is increasingly about high-quality proposals, semantic anatomy/detail, and in-engine judgment rather than basic ownership of mesh/material/rig/animation state.

This distinction must remain visible. Do not summarize the project as either "only research" or "AAA complete." Both would be false.
